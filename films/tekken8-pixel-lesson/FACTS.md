# Teaching scope
The film discusses general player habits, not a patch-specific tier list or character guide.
- Acting before an opponent's pressure ends can get interrupted; the lesson is to recognize turns, not to claim the defender must never challenge.
- A punish is guaranteed only when the opponent's recovery and the chosen response allow it. The animation explicitly marks an unsafe fictional move and uses no claimed game frame values.
- Practicing one stable punish and reviewing repeated mistakes are recommendations, not guarantees of a particular rank or win rate.
No precise statistics, performance promises or character-specific move data are stated.
